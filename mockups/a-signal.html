Explainer

How the Urge screen works

Three beats, under ten seconds. No quote, no lecture. The pause does the work; the button makes leaving the easy path.

Escalating friction: the delay grows as phone time approaches the 2h cap. 0 → 1h30 → 1h45

01

Delay

A breath ring holds the app for 5s. The feed is not shown until it ends.

02

Close it

One big button. "Open anyway" stays a small link, available but never the default.

03

Logged

Each close adds to today's tally (6/8) and to the Weekly Mirror. Opens are logged too, without judgement.

Research: Grüning, Riedel & Lorenz-Spreen (2023), PNAS 120(8): a one-second-plus delay with a dismiss option reduced app openings; dismissing was the most used and most effective path. Habit arc: Lally et al. (2010), median 66 days to automaticity; one missed day did not derail it.
