Why the water drains

The budget never blocks you outright until it is empty. It makes each reach slower, in steps you can see coming.

  1. Two hours, poured in at Fajr.Every minute in a watched app lowers the surface. The level is what you see each time you pick up the phone.
  2. Friction steps up as it drains.Above 30m left, a reach costs a 5s breath. Below 30m it is 15s, below 15m it is 30s. Empty leaves calls, Quran and maps.
  3. The body earns some back.A logged knee set (5 × 45s wall sit) returns 10 minutes, twice a day at most. The rehab becomes the currency.
